**Date:** 2026-09-24

Replaces the mock HomeScreen with the real one, per the "simplify to v1 model"
decision.

- Fetches `/client/home`: greeting with the member's name, plan status
  (membership/package, or "No active plan"), real **wallet balance** and
  **points** (tappable → wallet / rewards).
- **Upcoming classes** from the backend rendered as simplified cards (title,
  date + time, description, price or "Free"), with a **Book** action or a
  "Booked ✓" badge.

  won't cover it) or reserve & pay at the desk → `/client/classes/:id/book`;
  sonner toast on success/insufficient-wallet; refetches on success.

Dropped from the mock: instructor/rating/reviews/multi-slot/spots (not in the v1
class model). The old session-details/booking-confirm/success screens remain in
the tree but are no longer reached from Home.
